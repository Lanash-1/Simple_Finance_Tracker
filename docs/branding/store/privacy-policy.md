# Pocketsum Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 September 2026_

Pocketsum ("the app") is published by Codigitech. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **nothing leaves your device.**

## Data we collect
None. Pocketsum does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data or usage analytics. The app has no network access and no server component.

## Data stored on your device
Everything you enter (accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, recurring rules, settings, and an optional app-lock PIN) is stored in a local database on your device only. It is never uploaded anywhere.

## Permissions
- **Notifications** – to remind you when recurring transactions are added.
- **Biometrics** – to unlock the app with fingerprint or face, if you turn app lock on. Biometric data is handled entirely by the operating system; the app never sees it.
- **Run at startup** (Android) – to re-schedule reminders after the phone restarts.

## Backups
Android may include app data in your device backup according to your system settings. Pocketsum does not run its own backup or sync.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL with a new date.
